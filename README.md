# Alpha Presence

> Alpha Presence is a UK-based digital agency that helps UK local businesses fix their online presence. We work with trades, clinics, salons, cafés, gyms, independent retailers and small professional practices across the United Kingdom, building websites, improving user experience, automating admin work, and making businesses findable in both traditional search (Google, Bing) and AI answer engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, Copilot). Everything is explained in plain English, scoped up front, and quoted at a fixed price.

## Services

- **Website Design & Build** — A fast, modern site that turns searches into enquiries. Typical local business sites are £1,200–£4,500 and take 2–4 weeks.
- **UI/UX Design** — Clear journeys so visitors act instead of bouncing. Usually 1–2 weeks.
- **Automation** — Cut the admin: bookings, follow-ups and forms on autopilot. Most setups are live within 2 weeks.
- **Branding & SEO/AEO/GEO** — Get found on Google and named in AI answers, through structured content, schema markup, consistent business details and a matching Google Business Profile.

## How to book a consultation

Consultations are free. Use the "Book a free consultation" form on the homepage (name, email, phone, business name, the service you need and a short description of your situation) and Alpha Presence replies within one business day. Visitors who aren't sure what they need can choose "Not sure / something else" to reach a person directly by WhatsApp, phone, or a lightweight callback form.
